# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This document applies to all interactions in spaces associated with the Necookie AI project, including the issue tracker, pull requests, code review comments, and any other project-affiliated communication channel. It applies to everyone who participates — contributors, maintainers, and anyone reporting issues or asking questions.

---

## Standards

The following behaviors are expected of all participants:

- Communicate clearly and respectfully, even in disagreement
- Give constructive feedback focused on the work, not the person
- Accept criticism of your own contributions without taking it personally
- Stay on topic in technical discussions
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and move on

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Insults, personal attacks, or derogatory remarks directed at individuals or groups
- Publishing anyone's private information without their consent
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Deliberate intimidation or persistent disruption of discussion

---

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior can be sent to the project maintainer. All reports are reviewed confidentially. The maintainer will not disclose the identity of a reporter without their consent.

Depending on severity and context, consequences for violations range from a private warning to a permanent ban from the project. Repeated or serious violations are handled without prior warnings.

---

## Enforcement Process

1. **Warning** — A private written notice explaining the violation and what is expected going forward. No further action if the behavior stops.

2. **Temporary suspension** — Removal from all project interaction for a defined period. Applies to repeated or moderately serious violations.

3. **Permanent ban** — Applied for sustained violations, harassment of individuals, or behavior that is deliberately harmful to others in the community.
